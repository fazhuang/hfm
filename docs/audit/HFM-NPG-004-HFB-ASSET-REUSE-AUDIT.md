## 4. Coupling findings

1. HFB is a research workbench; the confirmed HFM direction also needs a visitor-first public portal.
2. HFB Evidence/SourceRef/Citation and version families overlap with but do not equal the frozen HFM canonical model.
3. HFB identity, RBAC, workspace, reports, AI, and admin functions are tightly coupled to HFB data and route semantics.
4. Elasticsearch/Redis/MinIO are HFB dependencies; Neo4j and AI libraries are optional extras. None becomes an HFM requirement by existence.
5. Software reuse is legally distinguishable from content reuse: `/apps` and `/packages` are MIT-licensed in addition to the repository license; data/media rights remain unproven.

## 5. Candidate-only conclusion

The highest-value candidates are adaptation of the Vue design system, Library/Reader interaction patterns, research workspace, evidence-aware reports, source admission, and security/test scenarios. Canonical Evidence/SourceRef/Citation data, if admitted, requires migration into HFM rather than schema reuse. The corpus, knowledge graph, AI runtime, and all static exhibition media remain unresolved or reference-only until customer assets, rights, public-product scope, and medical boundaries are confirmed.

No candidate in this report is a frozen reuse decision.
